# Store Policies

These policies apply to purchases made through our fictional online store. They describe standard shipping, sizing, returns, exchanges, and refund handling.

## Document Information

- **Document:** Store Policies
- **Scope:** Customer-facing policies for online orders
- **Version:** 1.0

## Shipping

### Processing and Dispatch

Standard orders are dispatched within **1–2 business days** after purchase. Dispatch is the point at which an order leaves our facility; it is separate from the time required for delivery.

### Delivery Estimates

Where available, the estimated delivery timeframe is shown during checkout. Delivery timing can vary by destination and carrier. The dispatch timeframe above does not include transit time.

### Tracking and Delays

Tracking information is provided after an order has been dispatched. Carrier tracking may take time to show its first update. If tracking does not update or an order appears delayed, contact customer support with the order number so the shipment can be reviewed.

## Sizing and Fit

### Size Charts

Use the size chart shown on the product page as a guide when selecting a size. Measurements and fit can vary between products, so check the chart for the specific item.

### Fit Guidance

If you are between sizes or need help interpreting a product's size chart, contact customer support before ordering. A fit concern after purchase may be handled through an eligible exchange or return under the rules below.

### Exchanges for Sizing

Eligible items that are not marked sale or final sale can be exchanged within **30 calendar days from the purchase date**. Contact customer support with the order number and item to request an exchange. Items marked sale or final sale cannot be exchanged because of fit or a change of mind.

## Returns and Exchanges

### Return Window

Standard returns are allowed when requested within **30 calendar days from the purchase date**. Calendar days include weekends and holidays. The window is measured from the purchase date, not the delivery date.

### General Return Conditions

The standard return window applies to items that are not marked sale or final sale. Sale and final-sale items cannot be returned for fit or a change of mind. Damaged, defective, or incorrectly fulfilled items can still be reported for review as described below, even when the standard return window or sale-item rule would otherwise prevent a return.

### How to Start a Return

Contact customer support with the order number, the item you want to return or exchange, and the reason for your request. Support will review the request and provide the next steps. Reporting an issue does not by itself mean that a return or refund has been approved.

### Return Shipping

For ordinary eligible returns, the customer pays the return shipping cost. For a damaged, defective, or incorrectly fulfilled item, the company covers return shipping when the case is approved after review. Support will provide the applicable return instructions.

## Sale Items

### Final-Sale Items

Items marked **Sale** or **Final Sale** are final sale. They cannot be returned or exchanged because of fit or a change of mind. This rule does not prevent a customer from reporting an item that is damaged, defective, or incorrectly fulfilled for review.

### Exceptions for Damaged or Incorrect Items

If a sale or final-sale item is damaged, defective, or is not the item ordered, contact customer support. The issue can be reported for review even though ordinary fit or change-of-mind returns are not accepted. Any return, exchange, or refund depends on the review outcome.

## Refunds

### Refund Eligibility

Refunds are issued only after the return or reported issue has been approved. An ordinary return must meet the 30-calendar-day return window and must not be for fit or a change of mind on an item marked sale or final sale. A damaged, defective, or incorrectly fulfilled item may be reported for review even when those standard rules would not otherwise allow a return.

### Refund Method

An approved refund is returned to the original payment method used for the purchase.

### Refund Timing

Approved refunds are processed within **5–7 business days after approval**. The processing period begins when the refund is approved, not when a return request is first submitted.

## Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Orders

### Reporting a Problem

Contact customer support to report an item that arrived damaged, is defective, or does not match the order. Include the order number and a description of the issue. These cases can be reported for review even when the standard return window has passed or the item is marked sale or final sale.

### Review and Resolution

The company reviews the report and communicates the available resolution. A report is not an automatic refund approval. If a return is approved for a damaged, defective, or incorrectly fulfilled item, the company covers the return shipping cost. Any approved refund is returned to the original payment method and processed within 5–7 business days after approval.

## Definitions

- **Purchase date:** The date the order was purchased. Return and exchange windows are measured from this date, rather than the delivery date.
- **Calendar day:** Any day on the calendar, including weekends and holidays.
- **Business day:** A weekday that is not a public holiday observed by the company.
- **Sale or final-sale item:** An item marked **Sale** or **Final Sale** on its product listing or order.
- **Incorrectly fulfilled item:** An item delivered that does not match the item in the customer's order.
